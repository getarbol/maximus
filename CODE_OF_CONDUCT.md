# Code of conduct

Maximus adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, as its code of conduct, for every space where the project is discussed: issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other project channel.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and
level of experience, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment and
personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

A private contact for conduct reports will be announced. Until then, open a short GitHub issue asking
a maintainer to contact you privately, without describing the incident, and a maintainer will reach
out. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers who are involved in an incident step aside
from handling it.
